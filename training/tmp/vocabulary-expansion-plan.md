# Vocabulary Expansion Plan

## Current Gaps Identified

### 1. Numbers — Only פֵּייָאק (one) and שְׁלֹשָׁה (three) exist
**Pattern to establish:** Everyday counting uses Algonquian; sacred/liturgical counting uses Hebrew.

Need to add:
- Algonquian 1-10 (everyday): peyak, niij, niswi, niiwin, naanan, ningodwaaswi, niizhwaaswi, nishwaaswi, zhaangaswi, midaaswi
- Hebrew numbers for sacred contexts: אֶחָד, שְׁנַיִם, שְׁלֹשָׁה, אַרְבָּעָה, חֲמִשָּׁה, שִׁשָּׁה, שִׁבְעָה, שְׁמוֹנָה, תִּשְׁעָה, עֲשָׂרָה
- Ordinals (Hebrew for liturgical, Algonquian for everyday)
- Examples showing when to use which

### 2. Days of the Week
**Pattern:** Hebrew names (following Jewish calendar tradition) for the seven days.
- יוֹם רִאשׁוֹן, יוֹם שֵׁנִי, יוֹם שְׁלִישִׁי, יוֹם רְבִיעִי, יוֹם חֲמִישִׁי, יוֹם שִׁשִּׁי, שַׁבָּת

### 3. Months of the Year
**Pattern:** Algonquian moon-names for the natural/agricultural calendar; Hebrew month names for religious calendar.

Algonquian moon names (seasonal, land-based):
- Spirit Moon, Bear Moon, Sugar Moon, Sucker Moon, Flower Moon, Strawberry Moon, etc.

Hebrew months for religious observance:
- תִּשְׁרֵי, חֶשְׁוָן, כִּסְלֵו, טֵבֵת, שְׁבָט, אֲדָר, נִיסָן, אִיָּר, סִיוָן, תַּמּוּז, אָב, אֱלוּל

### 4. Modern Terminology Patterns
**Pattern to establish:** Modern concepts follow these rules:
- If it's a physical object that could be described: Algonquian descriptive compound
- If it's an abstract concept from outside: Hebrew loan adaptation
- If it's international technical: adapt to Hebrew orthography

Need examples:
- Computer/technology terms
- Transportation (car, bus, train, airplane)
- Communication (email, internet, website)
- Medical terms
- Educational terms
- Government/civic terms

### 5. Colors
**Pattern:** Algonquian for natural/perceptual colors.
- waabishkaa (white), makade (black), miskwaa (red), ozaawi (yellow), ozhaawashko (blue/green), etc.

### 6. Weather & Seasons
**Pattern:** Algonquian for weather phenomena and seasons (land-based knowledge).
- Four seasons with Algonquian names
- Weather terms: rain, snow, wind, storm, cloud, etc.

### 7. Body Parts (expand)
**Pattern:** Mix — some already Hebrew, should show Algonquian alternatives for certain contexts.

### 8. Food & Cooking (expand)
**Pattern:** Algonquian for indigenous foods; Hebrew for foods brought from elsewhere.

### 9. Verbs of Daily Life (expand)
More Algonquian action verbs for physical activities.

### 10. Abstract Concepts (expand)
More Hebrew abstract nouns: justice, truth, wisdom, love, etc.

### 11. Question Words (expand)
Full set of interrogatives.

### 12. Demonstratives & Pronouns
Full pronoun paradigm, demonstratives beyond יוֹ/יוֹוָא.
