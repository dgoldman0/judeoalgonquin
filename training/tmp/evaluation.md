# Evaluation of Prototype Translations

This document analyzes each line from both songs against our unified Judeo-Algonquin grammar (from judeo-algonquin.md) and identifies adjustments needed for consistency.

---

## Key Patterns from the Unified Grammar

1. **Person Prefixes**: נְ־/נֶ־ (1st), קְ־/כְּ־ (2nd), וְ־ (3rd)
2. **Locative Suffix**: -ink/-ənk (וִיגְוָאמִינְק)
3. **Obviative Marker**: -וָא (-wa) for secondary 3rd person
4. **Proximate Index**: יוֹ (yo) - unmarked discourse center
5. **Wayyiqtol Chain**: וַיִּ- for sequential narrative
6. **Hebrew Clitics**: ב־, ל־, מ־, ו־, ה־ as discourse frames
7. **Algonquian Verbs**: Person-marked predicates (נֶמִּיצוּ, נוֹפּוּנְדַאוּוִין)
8. **Algonquian Nouns**: אֲפוּאָן (bread), מְבּוּי (water), תְּמָקָאן (path), לֵנוּוָאק (people)

## Summary of Needed Adjustments

### Consistent Vocabulary Substitutions

| Hebrew Form | Algonquian Replacement | Meaning |
|-------------|----------------------|---------|
| דֶּרֶךְ | תְּמָקָאן | path, road |
| הָעָם | לֵנוּוָאק | people |
| כּוֹסוֹת | מִנִיקְוָאגַנַאק | cups |
| בַּיִת | וִיגְוָאם | house |
| נֵר | וָאסָאקוֹנֵינְדִיגַן | candle |
| רוֹאִים (ptcp) | נֶוָאפּוּוֵין | we see |
| זוֹכְרִים (ptcp) | נֶמִּקְוֵינְדַאמֵין | we remember |

### Consistent Affixation

1. **Person prefix on Algonquian verbs**: Always נֶ- for 1st person plural
2. **Locative -ink**: Standardize spelling (not -ənk/-ank variations)
3. **Obviative -וָא**: Use consistently for secondary 3rd person in narrative

### Register Distinction

- **"We Walk Well"**: Community/liturgical register — more Algonquian predicates, fewer wayyiqtol chains
- **"Lights Learn Names"**: Narrative register — wayyiqtol-driven, Hebrew verbs with Algonquian vocabulary anchors

Both registers are valid; the final prototype.csv should show appropriate register for each line.

## Principles for Final Translation

1. **Algonquian verbs preferred** for: motion, perception, eating, giving, making
2. **Hebrew verbs acceptable** for: wayyiqtol narrative chains, abstract actions
3. **Algonquian nouns preferred** for: food, water, paths, houses, people, animals, tools
4. **Hebrew nouns acceptable** for: abstract concepts (תִּקְוָה, חֶסֶד, אוֹר), domestic objects in mixed registers
5. **Locatives (-ink)** on all place nouns in oblique positions
6. **Prox/Obv** only in narrative with multiple 3rd persons
7. **Person prefixes** on all Algonquian verbs
