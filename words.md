“Algonquin” can mean (1) **Algonquin (Omàmiwininìmowin / Anishinaabemowin)**, the language spoken in parts of Québec/Ontario, or (2) the wider **Algonquian** language family. Below is a big **Algonquin (language)** vocabulary set first, then additional **Algonquian-family** words from other languages, each labeled with **language + subfamily** (Central vs Eastern Algonquian). ([Wikipedia][1])

The focus of the vocabulary used for Judeo-Algonquin should preference vocabulary from speakers near the "Hudson Valley" region. This dictionary is simply used as a reference. As with the rest of Judeo-Algonquin it should be a solid blend of Hebrew and Algonquin esp. focusing on selecting where the thing the word represents is more important to one language over the other.
