# Domestic action tranche

**Status:** manually authored candidate language design; nothing here is
canonical

**Level:** N1 novice enrichment

**Scope:** eating, bringing, family reference, joint activity, static domestic
settings, and richer ordinary coordination

## Outcome

This tranche adds 39 structured records to the preceding 225-record corpus,
bringing the knowledge base to 264 noncanonical records. It contains five new
source-facing lexemes, five contact lexemes, four closed constructions, two
phrases, and twenty-three sentences. Codex selected and reviewed the lexical
allocations, contact morphophonemics, closed cells, meanings, exclusions, and
compositions directly. Code validates those decisions; it did not generate the
language inventory.

The work turns an existing lexical bootstrap into a small domestic grammar.
Regional TA/TI suppletion and Absolute architecture select Hebrew-derived food,
family, and person vocabulary; Hebrew `yaxad` then frames a regionally inflected
first-plural activity. Existing locative and coordination systems allow complete
clauses such as `kəmiičohna lexem bayitənk` “we including you eat bread in a
house” and `kələmatapiihna šulxanənk wə-kəmiičohna lexem` “we including you sit
at a table and eat bread.” This is structural co-parentage, not a word-by-word
choice from two donor lists.

## Continuity and retrieval preflight

The *We Walk Well* ledger made four needs especially relevant: eating in the
home, carrying bread or fruit, a small child bringing bread, and eating together
on the way. The tranche deliberately advances only meanings its evidence and
current grammar can support. `bring` is not relabeled `carry`; static house
location is not “our homes”; bare `yeled katan` is not the legacy definite noun
phrase; and a route relator cannot yet attach to an eating event. Those residual
gaps stay explicit in the continuity ledger.

Before authoring, local exact and semantic retrieval inspected existing records
near bread, fruit, cup, child, smallness, the Absolute, plurality, definiteness,
and person marking. The available semantic index contains the 180 records from
the static-place checkpoint, so it helped recover older neighbors but could not
contain the later motion or new domestic records. Structured lookup and direct
record comparison therefore remained the final consistency authority.

## Contact lexical allocation

- `miičii-` means consume one of the admitted ordinary food objects. It does not
  mean erode, exhaust, trouble, or eat without an overt object.
- `peešəw-` selects an admitted animate-class entity and means accompanied
  transfer toward a contextual destination. It does not mean invite, fetch,
  give, carry on the body, take away, or encode a recipient or path.
- `peel-` is the inanimate-selecting suppletive bring stem, with the same
  semantic restrictions. Its first objects are `lexem`, `pri`, and
  `tiihinčəw`.
- `yaxad` marks joint participation by every member of an already indexed
  first-plural subject in the same activity. It is neither ordinary `wə-`
  coordination nor a comitative, reciprocal, or narrative linker.
- `em` “mother” reuses the existing Hebrew donor at revision 2. It receives
  human animate class only in the declared local uses and says nothing about
  possession, number, or a general kinship paradigm.

## Nine-cell domestic Absolute

The construction reuses the source-informed contact Absolute architecture but
is exhaustive at nine finite cells: three predicates by third singular,
first-plural inclusive, and first-plural exclusive.

| Predicate and class | 3sg | 1pl inclusive | 1pl exclusive |
|---|---|---|---|
| eat TI `miičii-` | `miičow` | `kəmiičohna` | `nəmiičohna` |
| bring TA `peešəw-` | `peešəwaw` | `kəpeešəwahna` | `nəpeešəwahna` |
| bring TI `peel-` | `peelow` | `kəpeelohna` | `nəpeelohna` |

Contact `a` marks the admitted TA cells and contact `o` the admitted TI cells.
These are project class bridges, not free Munsee morphemes. Before `o`, final
`ii` of `miičii-` deletes: underlying `miičii~o~w` surfaces as `miičow`, not an
unwieldy `miičiio...` sequence. The analytical segmentation preserves the full
lexical stem so the repair remains visible rather than silently changing the
source record.

Third-singular clauses require a bare lexical subject—currently `adam`,
`yeled`, or `em`—before the predicate. First-plural clauses do not add an
independent pronoun. Every clause ends with one mandatory bare, nondefinite,
class-compatible object; count objects are singular and no object receives
plural marking. Current food objects are `lexem` and `pri`; TA bring admits
`adam` or `yeled`; TI bring admits `lexem`, `pri`, or `tiihinčəw`.

Representative clauses are:

| Judeo-Algonquin | Analytical reading | Idiomatic English |
|---|---|---|
| `yeled miičow lexem` | child eat.TI~ABS.IN~3 bread | A child eats bread. |
| `em miičow pri` | mother eat.TI~ABS.IN~3 fruit | A mother eats fruit. |
| `kəmiičohna lexem` | 1PL.INCL~eat.TI~ABS.IN bread | We, including you, eat bread. |
| `nəmiičohna lexem` | 1PL.EXCL~eat.TI~ABS.IN bread | We, excluding you, eat bread. |
| `yeled peelow tiihinčəw` | child bring.TI~ABS.IN~3 cup | A child brings a cup. |
| `kəpeešəwahna yeled` | 1PL.INCL~bring.TA~ABS.AN child | We, including you, bring a child. |
| `em peešəwaw yeled` | mother bring.TA~ABS.AN~3 child | A mother brings a child. |

The last clause has two third-person animate participants. Their roles are
recoverable only from this construction's fixed overt noun order. It supplies
neither direct/inverse marking nor proximate/obviative tracking and therefore
cannot be used as narrative participant grammar.

## Phrases and richer sentences

The exact phrase `yeled katan` means one physically small child, with noun
before an invariant property word. It does not create a general adjective
system, definiteness, youth, affection, comparison, or agreement. The phrase
enters `yeled katan peelow lexem`, an ordinary candidate meaning “a small child
brings bread.” The separate nominal coordination `em wə-yeled` means “a mother
and a child.”

Postclausal `yaxad` adds joint coactivity without changing clusivity:

- `kəmiičohna lexem yaxad` — we including you eat bread together;
- `nəmiičohna lexem yaxad` — we excluding you eat bread together;
- `kəmiičohna pri yaxad` — we including you eat fruit together; and
- `nəmiičohna pri yaxad` — we excluding you eat fruit together.

Existing static phrases may follow a complete eat clause:

- `kəmiičohna lexem bayitənk` — we including you eat bread in a house;
- `nəmiičohna lexem bayitənk` — we excluding you eat bread in a house;
- `kəmiičohna lexem šulxanənk` — we including you eat bread at a table; and
- `yeled miičow lexem bayitənk` — a child eats bread in a house.

Finally, ordinary `wə-` coordinates complete, matching-clusivity clauses:

- `kələmatapiihna šulxanənk wə-kəmiičohna lexem` — we including you sit at a
  table and eat bread;
- `kəmiičohna lexem wə-kəməneehna` — we including you eat bread and drink.

The second example deliberately preserves different valencies: eating remains
objectful and drinking remains objectless. Neither linear order asserts “then,”
“therefore,” simultaneity, habituality, or a meal script.

## Narrative and productivity firewall

Nothing here implements past or future time, aspect, modality, polarity,
imperatives, definiteness, possession, object incorporation, a general noun
class system, direct/inverse alignment, or obviation. Ordinary `wə-` and
coactivity `yaxad` are distinct from both the planned wayyiqtol-derived
foreground chain and the planned weqatal-derived consequence or recurring
frame. The required wayyiqtol–weqatal–obviation synthesis remains a later,
independently sourced design task.

The tables and host lists are closed. Deterministic tests reject an unlisted
object, a TA/TI mismatch, object omission, an untruncated eat surface, reversed
`katan yeled`, singular coactivity, a directional phrase used as a static
setting, clusivity mismatch, or an inserted narrative reading.

## Embedding state and cost boundary

No API call was made for this tranche. The living local database still contains
180 fresh record vectors, so 45 motion/action records and these 39 domestic
records are absent from semantic retrieval. Exact structured lookup covers all
264 records.

A separately implemented dry checkpoint can add only the frozen 45-record
motion tranche plus twelve diagnostics in one 57-input request. Its rough
estimate is 34,692 input tokens, about **$0.000694**; its deliberately
conservative ceiling is 140,321 tokens, about **$0.002807**. It does not include
the newer domestic tranche. The 39 domestic embedding documents themselves are
about 103,940 UTF-8 bytes, roughly 25,985 tokens by the planning heuristic, or
about **$0.000520** before diagnostic queries. A later living-index plan should
add domestic-specific English, conlang, compositional, and contrastive queries
and prefer one bounded incremental refresh rather than treating these estimates
as permission to spend.

Paid access remains disabled and the prior authorization remains consumed. No
